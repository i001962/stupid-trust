What this does — and doesn't — prove

  • It proves: the other phone is in your group and just answered a fresh check.
  • It doesn't prove: who someone is outside your group, or a government ID. You still have to trust the invite step.
  • If someone gets your invite and your phone, they can pass. That's why an invite must come from someone you trust, and why a leaked group should be replaced, not reused.
  • “No signal” just means not verified yet. The person may be offline or not on this screen.
